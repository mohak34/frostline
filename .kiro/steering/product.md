# Product

Winter Water Watch tracks road-salt contamination of Massachusetts public drinking water wells.

## Users

- Residents on town water: "Is my water getting saltier?"
- Town water and health officials: "Which wells need attention?"

## Thresholds

- MassDEP sodium guideline: 20 mg/L (for people on sodium-restricted diets).
- MassDOT remediation triggers:
  - Chloride >= 250 mg/L.
  - Sodium > 20 mg/L with a doctor-documented diet under 1,000 mg/day.
  - Sodium > 40 mg/L with a doctor-documented diet under 2,000 mg/day.

## Rules

- No "unsafe by year X" countdowns.
- Label each well rising, flat, or falling, plus a "watch" flag.
- Publish our own backtest accuracy.
- No private wells. The data covers public water systems only.

## Pages

- Home: Plymouth well chart as the hook, plus town search.
- Town page: well charts, verdict, what to do, MassDOT checker.
- State map: towns by % of wells rising, environmental justice toggle, printable report.
- Accuracy page: backtest results.
