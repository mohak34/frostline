# Video script: Frostline + Harbor Pulse

Target length: 2:45 (hard limit 3:00). No music.

---

## 0:00 - 0:15 | Intro

> **SCREEN:** Frostline home page, map time-lapse playing.

**SAY:** "Massachusetts winters hit our water in two ways. Road salt seeps into the wells towns drink from, and nor'easters push the harbor into the streets, sometimes in freezing air. We built two tools, one for each, both with Kiro."

---

## 0:15 - 1:35 | Frostline

### 0:15 | The map

> **SCREEN:** Time-lapse map. Hover over a few towns.

**SAY:** "This is Frostline. It reads every sodium test the state has published for public wells and fits a trend to each one: 5,672 wells, and 856 of them are getting saltier. The map plays it back year by year, each town shaded by the share of wells over 20 milligrams per liter. That's the level the state advises for people on low-sodium diets."

### 0:40 | Search

> **SCREEN:** Type **Amherst** in the search. Map zooms in and glides into the town page.

**SAY:** "Search your town. The map zooms in and glides into the town page."

### 0:48 | Town page

> **SCREEN:** The four town facts, then scroll down to the wells table. The first chart opens by itself.

**SAY:** "You get the facts that matter: how many wells are over the line, how many are rising, and which ones are under it now but on track to pass it. Every well has its own history."

### 1:02 | Checker

> **SCREEN:** Scroll to the checker. Pick a well, pick a diet, the gauges move.

**SAY:** "The checker compares any well, or your own test results, against the state's road-salt triggers for your diet, and tells you why you did or didn't hit one."

### 1:15 | Accuracy

> **SCREEN:** Click **Accuracy** in the header.

**SAY:** "We tested our own warning. We hid every result after 2015 and checked it against what actually happened. It caught 49 of the 160 wells that crossed the line, and we publish that number."

---

## 1:35 - 2:20 | Harbor Pulse

### 1:35 | Switch over

> **SCREEN:** Click **Harbor Pulse** in the Frostline header. The forecast page loads.

**SAY:** "Harbor Pulse covers the coast. It's a 72-hour flood forecast for Boston Harbor, built on NOAA's tide gauge and live weather forecasts."

### 1:48 | Forecast

> **SCREEN:** The forecast chart and the flood odds.

**SAY:** "It predicts how far the water will rise above the normal tide, gives the odds of passing flood stage, and raises a Flood plus Freeze warning when floodwater is likely to meet freezing air, which is when flooding turns into ice."

### 2:05 | 2018 replay

> **SCREEN:** The January 4, 2018 nor'easter replay.

**SAY:** "You can also replay the record nor'easter of January 4th, 2018, hour by hour, and see what the warning would have shown."

---

## 2:20 - 2:45 | Kiro + close

### 2:20 | Kiro

> **SCREEN:** GitHub: the `.kiro` folder of both repos. Open a spec and a steering file.

**SAY:** "Both projects were built spec-first in Kiro. Requirements, design and tasks came before any code, and steering files held the data quirks and the rules. Kiro ran the task lists, and Kiro CLI added hooks and property-based tests at the end."

### 2:35 | Close

> **SCREEN:** Back on the Frostline home page.

**SAY:** "Salt in the ground, water in the streets. Both are public data nobody reads. Now you can see them. Frostline and Harbor Pulse."

---

## Recording tips

- Record each section separately, then join them. One take is harder.
- Open Amherst once before recording so the map and charts are already loaded.
- Check the Harbor Pulse lines against its screens. They come from its README.
- No music.
